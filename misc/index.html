Miscellaneous

Various other activities and sharing

I love imagining the practical impact AI could have in the future, and I’m also curious about how AI might reshape the world. Such interesting open questions!
When I'm not thinking about research, you might find me outdoors: hiking, long walks along a promenade, cycling, etc or just sitting by a river watching the water go by.
Other times, I slow down with a good book, a painting, a piece of classical music.
Know a trail, a book, or a concerto worth trying? I'd love a suggestion!
